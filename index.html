📞 Call Recording Guide
How to use:
1. Start your Zoom/Teams/Meet call
2. Tap "REC CALL" button in app
3. When prompted, tap "Share" and select your screen
4. Audio from call will record (both voices)
5. Tap "STOP REC" when done
6. Recording auto-downloads as .wav file
